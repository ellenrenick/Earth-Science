One CFA per learning target, in the Canvas sandbox (300209), all unpublished. Each CFA is a 5-question, self-graded New Quiz (5 pts, 1 attempt). Each has a Mastery Path: under 67.5% (below a B; 3 or fewer of 5 correct) releases that target's review assignment. 4 or 5 correct releases nothing.

## Review "Show what you know" keys

- 3.1: P = push-pull, S = side to side; P arrives first (fastest); at the liquid outer core P keeps going (slows, bends), S stops.
- 3.2: Station 2 (115°) is in the shadow zone (no waves); Station 3 shows S-waves were stopped, so the outer core is liquid; CER uses Stations 1 and 3 (and/or 2).
- 3.3: crust, mantle, outer core, inner core; outer and inner core are iron and nickel, outer core is liquid; deeper = hotter and denser.
- 3.4: heat left from formation and radioactive decay; explanation of either; the sun only heats the surface.
- 3.5: loop labeled core, rising warm rock, sinking cool rock; warm rock is less dense so it rises, cool rock is denser so it sinks; moves heat outward, cycles rock, moves plates.
