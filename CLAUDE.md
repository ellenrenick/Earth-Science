## Course facts

- Calendar: Semester 1 = 83 instructional days + 3 final exam days; Semester 2 = 91 instructional days + 3 final exam days. 50-minute periods.
- Textbook: McGraw Hill Inspire Earth Science. Each unit has 2-4 assigned reading sections (see the scope & sequence doc).
- Optional ExploreLearning Gizmo per unit for fill-in or extra work; Gizmos are not in the pacing.
- Scoring: 0-4 per learning target. Bands: 0.9 Not yet meeting, 1.8 Approaching, 2.7 Proficient, 3.5 Mastered.
